# Hotel & Hospitality Reservation Platform Database Architecture & Data Model

## Problem Overview
Designing a robust relational database schema for an online hotel and vacation rental reservation platform (such as Airbnb or Booking.com). The platform manages multi-property listings, room type categorizations, day-by-day room inventory and availability, dynamic calendar-based pricing, rule-based price increments, reservation lifecycle tracking, and bidirectional guest and host reviews.

## Business Context & Key Questions
The data model is engineered to support high-concurrency booking transactions and answer core operational and analytical questions:
1. **Real-time Room Availability:** Is a specific property or room type available for a customer's requested check-in and check-out dates?
2. **Revenue Performance:** What is the total monthly revenue generated by each property from confirmed bookings?
3. **Pending Reservation Backlog:** How many bookings are currently pending confirmation from hosts or platform validation?
4. **Guest Satisfaction & Quality Assurance:** What is the average rating score given by guests across completed stays?
5. **Demand-Driven Dynamic Pricing:** How can occupancy rates and historical demand levels trigger rule-based price increments?

## Key Requirements & System Constraints
* **Property & Room Hierarchies:** Support multi-room properties where each property can list multiple distinct room types (e.g., Deluxe Suite, Standard King, Ocean View).
* **Calendar-Based Availability Tracking:** Manage daily available room inventory per room type to prevent overbooking (`available_rooms >= 0`).
* **Multi-Currency Dynamic Pricing:** Store daily base rates per room type across multiple currency formats with unique calendar constraints.
* **Rule-Based Pricing Increments:** Define configurable rules (e.g., weekend surcharges, peak-season multipliers, high-demand surges) to adjust baseline pricing dynamically.
* **Strict Reservation Lifecycle & Integrity:** Enforce chronological consistency on booking dates (`check_out > check_in`) and track status states (`PENDING`, `CONFIRMED`, `CANCELLED`, `COMPLETED`).
* **Bidirectional Guest-Host Reviews:** Allow both guests and hosts to submit ratings (constrained strictly between `1` and `5`) and reviews post-checkout.


## 1. Business Process Flow Diagram

![Business Process Flow](Business_Process_Flow.png)

### Workflow Overview:
The Business Process Flow illustrates the complete end-to-end guest journey from initial search to post-stay feedback:
1. **Discovery & Search:** Guest visits the platform → Searches and filters properties by destination, dates, and guest capacity.
2. **Property & Room Selection:** Guest selects a property → Chooses desired room type (e.g., Deluxe Suite).
3. **Availability & Pricing Verification:** System checks real-time calendar availability for the selected dates → Computes applicable dynamic rate based on active pricing rules and currency.
4. **Booking Application & Details:** Guest applies for booking → Enters guest personal and contact details.
5. **Payment & Confirmation:** Guest completes payment → Reservation status transitions to confirmed.
6. **Stay & Review Submission:** Guest completes the stay → Both guest and host submit mutual ratings and reviews.


## 2. Logical Data Model

![Logical Data Model](Logical_Data_Model.png)

### Logical Design Overview:
The Logical Data Model defines the conceptual entities, business identifiers (`PK`), and key operational attributes without platform-specific physical foreign key constraints:
* **Guest Profile Management:** `Guest` (Captures personal details, contact info, account status, and joining date).
* **Property & Accommodation Structure:** `Property`, `Room_Type` (Defines properties, geographic locations, and room categories).
* **Inventory & Calendar Availability:** `Room_Type_Availability` (Maintains daily available room counts).
* **Pricing & Dynamic Rules Engine:** `Pricing`, `Pricing_Rule` (Manages daily currency rates and surge increment rules).
* **Reservation Processing:** `Booking` (Captures reservation dates, status, booking amounts, and guest links).
* **Feedback & Quality Assessment:** `Review` (Stores bidirectional review ratings and submission timestamps).


## 3. Entity Relationship Diagram (Physical Data Model)

![Entity Relationship Diagram](Entity_Relationship_Diagram.png)

### Relational Architecture & Foreign Keys:
The Entity Relationship Diagram (ERD) establishes the physical database schema, table cardinalities, primary keys (`PK`), and foreign key constraints (`FK`):
* **Property to Room Types:** `room_type` references `property` (`property_id`) via a 1-to-many relationship (`fk_roomtype_property`).
* **Room Availability Tracking:** `room_type_availability` references `room_type` (`room_type_id`) with a composite unique constraint on `(room_type_id, date)` (`fk_availability_roomtype`).
* **Daily Pricing Matrix:** `pricing` links to `room_type` (`room_type_id`) with a unique constraint on `(room_type_id, date, currency_type)` (`fk_pricing_roomtype`).
* **Dynamic Pricing Rules:** `pricing_rule` references `room_type` (`room_type_id`) to apply incremental price adjustments (`fk_pricingrule_roomtype`).
* **Booking Pipeline:** `booking` joins `guest` (`guest_id`), `property` (`property_id`), and `room_type` (`room_type_id`) while enforcing `check_out > check_in`.
* **Multi-Party Reviews:** `review` maintains foreign key references to `property` (`property_id`), `guest` (`guest_id`), and `booking` (`booking_id`), enforcing valid ratings between 1 and 5.


## Schema Architecture

The physical relational database model consists of 7 core tables:

| Table Name | Description | Key Attributes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`guest`** | Master profile registry for registered guests | `guest_id`, `name`, `email_id`, `phone_number`, `gender`, `is_active`, `joined_at` |
| **`property`** | Property listings and geographic location details | `property_id`, `property_name`, `location` |
| **`room_type`** | Room categories available within specific properties | `room_type_id`, `property_id`, `type` |
| **`room_type_availability`** | Real-time calendar room counts per room type per day | `room_type_availability_id`, `room_type_id`, `date`, `available_rooms` |
| **`pricing`** | Base daily pricing rates per room type and currency | `pricing_id`, `room_type_id`, `date`, `cost`, `currency_type` |
| **`pricing_rule`** | Rule-based dynamic pricing modifiers and surge increments | `pricing_rule_id`, `room_type_id`, `rule_type`, `increment_price` |
| **`booking`** | Reservation records, date intervals, status, and financials | `booking_id`, `room_type_id`, `property_id`, `guest_id`, `status`, `check_in`, `check_out`, `booking_amount` |
| **`review`** | Bidirectional ratings and reviews submitted post-stay | `review_id`, `property_id`, `guest_id`, `booking_id`, `rating`, `rater`, `given_at` |


## Key Business Queries

All operational and analytical SQL queries answering the primary business questions are provided in [`Business_Solution.sql`](Business_Solution.sql).

### Key Queries Included in `Business_Solution.sql`:
1. **Property Availability Check:** Evaluates whether specific room types at a property (e.g., *Taj Heights*) have available room inventory across requested check-in and check-out dates.
2. **Monthly Revenue per Property:** Computes aggregated monthly revenue earned per property for all confirmed reservations using `EXTRACT(MONTH / YEAR)`.
3. **Pending Bookings Backlog:** Counts the volume of reservations currently in `PENDING` status awaiting host or admin action.
4. **Guest Satisfaction Analytics:** Computes the average review score and feedback volume submitted by guests (`rater = 'GUEST'`).
5. **Dynamic Pricing Demand Analysis:** Calculates occupancy/demand percentages by comparing booked rooms against total capacity and assigns demand tiers (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`) to drive dynamic pricing adjustments.
